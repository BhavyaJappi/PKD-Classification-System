Introduction
• Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD) is a genetic disorder where many fluid-filled
cysts grow in the kidneys.
• These cysts cause the kidneys to enlarge, reducing their function and sometimes
leading to kidney failure.
• Traditional diagnosis depends on manual MRI/CT image analysis and genetic
testing, which is slow and time-consuming.
• Such methods are subjective and often detect the disease in late stages, missing
early warning signs.
• Using Machine Learning (ML), we can automate cyst detection, classify disease
severity, and predict disease progression.

Analysis of Literature Survey
Deep Learning Techniques: Most studies used CNN-based and U-Net models for
kidney and cyst segmentation, showing high accuracy but limited generalization.
Explainable AI (XAI): Recent research started using SHAP and LIME to make AI
models more interpretable and trustworthy for clinicians.
Data Limitations: Many studies used small or single-center datasets, which cause
bias and reduce the model’s performance on real-world data.
Lack of Multi-Modal Systems: Existing works focus on only imaging or gene data,
missing the benefit of combining imaging, genetic, and clinical features.
Need for Long-Term Prediction: Most models analyze data from a single time
point; there is a strong need for longitudinal or time-series analysis to track
disease progression.
Research Gap
1. Limited Early Detection Models:
Most existing studies focus on detecting PKD at later stages. There is a lack of machine
learning models that can identify early signs of the disease before severe kidney damage
occurs.
2. Lack of Explainability and Trust:
Many deep learning models work like “black boxes”, providing results without explaining
how decisions are made.This reduces trust among doctors and limits clinical adoption.
3. Insufficient Multi-Modal Integration:
Current research often uses only image data (MRI/CT) or only genetic data. There is a
research gap in combining both imaging and clinical/genetic information to make the
diagnosis more accurate and personalized.
Problem Definition
Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD) diagnosis currently depends on manual image
analysis and genetic testing, which are time-consuming and often detect the disease
at later stages. Existing AI models mainly focus on imaging data and lack early
detection capabilities and interpretability. Therefore, there is a need for a machine
learning–based system that can automatically detect cysts, assess disease severity,
and provide early, accurate, and explainable PKD diagnosis to support clinical
decision-making.
